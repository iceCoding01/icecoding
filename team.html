The people behind ICECODING

We're a small, focused team of technologists who care deeply about solving real problems. Everyone here contributes directly to the quality of our work.

How we work together

We started ICECODING because we believed the technology industry needed more honesty. We wanted to build a place where smart people could do their best work without the politics or pretense.

Our team is small by design. We believe quality work comes from deep collaboration and accountability. Every person here matters to our mission—there are no roles that exist just to fill an org chart.

We hire for capability and character. We've found that curious people who think rigorously and communicate clearly can learn almost any technology. What matters more is whether they'll push back on bad ideas and support the team when the work gets hard.
